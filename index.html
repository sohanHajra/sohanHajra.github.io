  1. 01

    From Signal to Fill

    When market conditions are matched, does extra delay between a trading decision and its order’s outcome cost price or fills?

    Hypothesis
    Extra delay from 1 ms to 500 ms raises a taker’s execution shortfall and lowers a passive order’s chance of filling.
    Test setup
    The same decision replayed at nine delays on Binance BTCUSDT perpetual data, preregistered, two locked test days run once plus six days for breadth.
    Verdict
    Inconclusive Both primary tests are null, largely by design. A taker acting on book imbalance paid 0.52 to 0.66 bps by 500 ms, an upper end.

How much should you bet on an edge?

Each line is one bankroll making the same repeated bet, staking a fixed fraction each time. Bet the Kelly fraction and growth is fastest; bet more and the paths get wilder, until around twice Kelly the typical bankroll stops growing at all.

Agent evals

Graders that check the work, not the report
  1. E1

    Handoff Drift Eval

    Agent evaluation · 2026

    Does a coding agent actually finish a multi-file change, or only say that it did?

    A small agent task with its grader. The agent migrates a fill-reconciliation pipeline from float dollars to integer cents across two files, then regenerates the books and a handoff manifest. The grader ignores the agent's report: it runs the agent's pipeline on a holdout batch in a separate process and checks the files left on disk.

    A reference solution passes and eight deliberately broken variants fail. The README lists what the grader does not catch.
