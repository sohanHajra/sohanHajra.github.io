How much should you bet on an edge?

Each line is one bankroll making the same repeated bet, staking a fixed fraction each time. Bet the Kelly fraction and growth is fastest; bet more and the paths get wilder, until around twice Kelly the typical bankroll stops growing at all.

Bankroll, log scale · 0 betsen·g(f)at Kelly, for comparison

Growth g(f) against fraction f

  • Kelly fraction f*–

g(f) = p ln(1 + b f) + q ln(1 − f)expected log growth per bet

Simulated coin flips; the same random draws are reused as you move the sliders, so you see the effect of the fraction, not of new luck. Reset draws new ones.
