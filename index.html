Research principles

What I optimize for.

The recurring theme across my work is not a specific model. It is trying to make the path from idea to evidence harder to fool.

01

Separate selection from evaluation

Use training and validation data to make choices. Keep the final test set genuinely out of the decision loop.

02

Benchmark before celebrating

A strategy or model only becomes interesting relative to a credible baseline, realistic costs, and the risk it takes.

03

Explain the miss

When the result disappoints, attribution and failure analysis often teach more than another round of parameter optimization.

About
