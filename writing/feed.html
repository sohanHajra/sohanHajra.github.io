  1. Sequel · 25 Nov 2026
    01.b · Signal Cost Confirm (sequel to 01)

    Two leads from 01, checked once on twelve unseen days with the rules written first. Verdict: Supported for both. A taker acting on an extreme imbalance signal paid more the longer its order took, with the slope interval above zero on all 12 days and an extra 0.48 to 0.68 bps by 500 ms. The passive effect (C2) holds but is small.

  2. Proposal · 22 Nov 2026
    01 · From Signal to Fill

    When market conditions are matched, does extra delay between a trading decision and its order's outcome cost price or fills? Verdict: Inconclusive. Both primary tests are null, largely by design. A taker acting on order book imbalance paid 0.52 to 0.66 bps by 500 ms on the test days, an upper end.
